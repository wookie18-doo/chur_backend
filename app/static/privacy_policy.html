Bank statements are not stored. When you upload a statement, we send it to an AI processing provider (see section 4) to extract the transactions, then discard the file. We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data, or your advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID), and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Retention, security and deleting your account

Retention. We keep your data while your account is active. After you delete your account, we erase your personal and financial data within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days. Records we must keep by law (such as invoices) are kept for the legally required period. Crash logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Uploaded statements are held in memory only for the time needed to process them, typically seconds, and are not written to our database, file storage, logs or backups.

Security. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised personnel who need it to operate the Service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  2. On the web, without the app: https://chur.wookie18.com/delete-account.
  3. By email to info@wookie18.com.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. Cancel any active subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play settings; deleting your account does not cancel it automatically.

Data stored only on your device. If you use Chur without an account, your data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to receive it in a portable format (CSV export is available in Settings → Export data), and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any right, email info@wookie18.com; we reply within 30 days.

You may also complain to the Slovenian supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (www.ip-rs.si), or to the authority in your country. Users in California, the UK and elsewhere have equivalent rights, which we honour on the same terms.

7. Children
